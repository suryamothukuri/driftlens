⚡ How Semantic Drift Works

1

Ingest & Localize

Pull raw versioned document layers (Item 1A risk disclosures) with strict rate control and immutable Bronze checkpoints.

2

Embed & Discover

Sentence embeddings via local BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5, followed by UMAP projection and global HDBSCAN theme discovery.

3

Centroid Drift

Compute year-over-year cosine divergence between theme embeddings to isolate semantic phrasing shifts from raw word-count diffs.

4

Evidence Grounding

Synthesize concise explanations strictly citing extracted source excerpts — zero hallucinations or speculative invention.
